## English (en-US), default language

**Title:** Gym Reps: Set & Rest Timer

**Short description:** Count your sets with one tap and never miss the end of your rest.

**Full description:**

Gym Reps is a simple set counter and rest timer for the gym. Tap the big button when you finish a set, rest, and the app tells you when it's time for the next one.

ONE TAP PER SET
• Tap to start, tap after each set, and Gym Reps counts for you
• Set time and total workout time on screen
• Undo a set or reset with one tap

REST TIMER THAT REACHES YOU
• Pick your rest from presets (45 s, 1 min, 1 min 30 s…) or add your own
• The timer keeps running in the notification and on the lock screen
• Sound and vibration the moment your rest is over, even with the screen off

WORKOUT GROUPS
• Put exercises together, like "Push day" or "Leg day"
• Start the group and Done takes you to the next exercise
• Reorder by holding and dragging

HISTORY
• Every exercise saved with sets, time, rest and notes
• Grouped by day, with start and end times
• Add a photo of the machine so you remember the settings
• Edit or delete any entry

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Everything stays on your phone

Free for your first 150 saved exercises. After that, a one-time unlock removes the reminder for good and supports development. No subscription.

Available in English, Italian and Spanish.

## Play Console steps

1. **Store listing languages.** Play Console → Gym Reps → *Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing*. Fill in English (the default). Then click **Manage translations → Add your own translations**, tick **Italian – it-IT** and **Spanish (Spain) – es-ES**, and click **Apply**.
2. **Paste the texts.** Use the language picker at the top of the listing page to switch to each language and paste its title, short and full description from above. **Save** each one.
3. **Screenshots per language (optional).** In each language there is a *Graphics* section. Leave it empty to reuse the English images, or upload localized screenshots there. If you want them, I can take Italian and Spanish screenshots on the emulator with demo data.
4. **Optional: Latin American Spanish.** To reach Mexico and other countries, also add **Spanish (Latin America) – es-419** and paste the same Spanish text. The app itself already shows Spanish to every Spanish phone.
5. **In-app product.** *Monetize → Products → In-app products → gymreps_full*: add Italian and Spanish name and description from the table above. Save, then make sure it's still **Active**.
6. **Release.** Upload the new AAB with the languages (versionCode 2 or higher) to a testing track, check it, then promote to production. The listing text can be saved and published on its own, without a new build.
7. **Checking.** On a phone with Android 13 or newer, the app also appears under *Settings → Apps → Gym Reps → Language*, offering English, Italiano and Español.
