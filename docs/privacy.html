NeonRep: Gym Rest Timer – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

English Italiano Español

NeonRep keeps everything on your phone. It has no account, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We do not collect, share or sell any personal data.

Data you enter

Exercise names, sets, reps, rest times, notes, history, groups and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Exercise photos

You can optionally add a photo to an exercise, either by taking one with your camera app or by choosing one from your gallery through Android's photo picker. The app only receives the single photo you pick. It is copied into the app's private storage on your phone and is never uploaded.

Purchases

The optional one-time "full version" unlock is processed by Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. The app only receives a confirmation from Google Play that the unlock was purchased, so it can enable the full version.

Notifications, alarms and the timer service

  • Notifications show the running rest timer and tell you when a rest is over.
  • Exact alarms make sure the "rest over" alert arrives on time even when the phone is idle. If you decline this permission, the app uses a less precise alarm.
  • A foreground service keeps the timer running while the app is in the background.

None of these features send any data off your device.

Backup

If you have turned on Android's device backup in your Google account settings, Android may include the app's data in that backup. This is controlled by you and by Google, not by the app.

Deleting your data

You can delete history entries and photos inside the app. To remove everything, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
